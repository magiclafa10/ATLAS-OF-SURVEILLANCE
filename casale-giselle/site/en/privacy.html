5. Your rights

At any time you can ask the controller, by writing to the email in section 1, to:

  • see what data we hold about you and get a copy;
  • correct or update it;
  • delete it, unless the law requires us to keep it;
  • restrict its use or object to processing based on legitimate interest;
  • receive it in a readable format (portability);
  • withdraw any consent you gave, without affecting processing already carried out.

We reply within one month. If you believe your data is being handled incorrectly, you can lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (garanteprivacy.it).

6. Cookie policy

This website does not use profiling, advertising or analytics cookies. We don't ask for consent or show a banner: under the Italian Data Protection Authority's cookie guidelines, a site that uses at most technical tools doesn't need a banner, and this notice is enough.
